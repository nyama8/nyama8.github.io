I am an [IC Postdoctoral Fellow](https://orise.orau.gov/icpostdoc/index.html) at the University of California, Berkeley in the [Quantum Devices Group](https://quantumdevicesgroup.github.io/), headed by [Prof. Alp Sipahigil](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Faculty/Homepages/alp.html).
My current research focuses primarily on the theory and development of novel nonlinear electro-optic devices for applications in quantum transduction.

I received my Ph.D. in electrical engineering from the University of Washington in 2025, where I worked on hybrid-integrated photonics for quantum networking with color centers in diamond in the [Quantum Defect Lab](https://sites.google.com/uw.edu/optospintronics-lab/home) headed by [Prof. Kai-Mei Fu](https://people.ece.uw.edu/fu_kaimei/).
I received my B.S. in electrical engineering with a minor in physics from the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa in 2020.
