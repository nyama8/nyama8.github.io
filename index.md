I am an IC Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of California, Berkeley, working with Prof. Alp Sipahigil on the theory, design, and experimental investigation of novel devices with enhanced electro-optic nonlinearities for quantum transduction.

I received my PhD in electrical engineering from the University of Washington in 2025, where I was a member of the [Quantum Defect Lab](https://sites.google.com/uw.edu/optospintronics-lab/home) headed by [Prof. Kai-Mei Fu](https://people.ece.uw.edu/fu_kaimei/).
My doctoral research focused on the modeling, engineering, and realization of hybrid-integrated photonic elements for quantum networking with color centers in diamond.
More broadly, my research interests lie at the intersection of the physics and engineering of quantum systems; spanning from investigations of the underlying physics, to the modeling and realization of quantum technologies.

In the spring of 2020, I graduated from the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa with a BS in electrical engineering and minor in physics.
While an undergraduate, I was a member of the Liquid Metal Electronics Lab co-advised by [Prof. Wayne Shiroma](https://ee.hawaii.edu/faculty/profile?usr=25) and [Prof. Aaron Ohta](https://ee.hawaii.edu/faculty/profile?usr=62).
I also spent two summers working under [Prof. Vinod Menon](https://www.ccny.cuny.edu/profiles/vinod-menon-0) of the City College of New York's [Laboratory for Nano and Microphotonics (LaNMP)](http://lanmp.org/), one of which as a participant of Columbia University's [MRSEC REU](https://mrsec.columbia.edu/reu).

I am very fortunate to be a recipient of the 2020 [National Science Foundation (NSF) Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/). While an undergraduate at the University of Hawaii, I was a [Regents Scholar](https://www.hawaii.edu/offices/student-affairs/regents-and-presidential-scholars-program/).
