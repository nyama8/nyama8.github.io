My research interests broadly lie at the intersection of the physics and engineering of quantum systems, spanning from investigations of the underlying physics to the modeling and realization of quantum technologies.
More specifically, my current focus is quantum optics and nonlinear photonics, and how such physics can be leveraged to achieve new and/or improved functionality in classical and quantum devices.
I am particularly excited by novel approaches to long-standing problems wherein new, or often overlooked, physical insights enable large gains in both practicality and performance.
